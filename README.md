# F6 SP Lesson 7 - DSE 2012 & 2013 Paper 1: True / False / Not Given

Helen's F.6 English SP lesson pack on the **True / False / Not Given** question type.
No vocabulary and no exam tips: the lesson is the exam questions only, each with the
complete exam paragraph(s) printed underneath for students to read. Students answer
**DSE style - blacken ONE circle** for each statement.

* Answer format: three circles per statement (T / F / NG); students blacken one, as in the
  DSE Question-Answer Book. The two 2012 questions, whose paper printed "Put a tick in the
  appropriate column", use the same circle instruction for consistency across the lesson.
* Q1: statement "You need good English to play games imported from Germany or the US."
  is not used, so the question is scored out of 3 (the printed examination instruction
  still says 4 marks in the original paper).
* Q3: statement (iv) of the original paper was deleted by HKEAA, so the published
  question is scored out of 3.
* Q6 prints the whole of paragraph 4 - both of its column halves, including the
  "Internet discussion boards" sentence - and paragraph 5 beneath.
* Percentages are the proportion of DSE candidates who got each statement right.
